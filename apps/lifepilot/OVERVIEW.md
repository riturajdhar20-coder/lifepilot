# LifePilot

A private personal hub for individuals to keep track of everyday life admin: tasks, bills, document expiry dates (e.g. passport, insurance), and appointments.

- **Audience:** members of the public who sign up with their email.
- **Privacy:** every record belongs to the signed-in person and is only visible to them. Users can erase all their LifePilot data from Settings.
- **Scope:** names, dates and notes only — no document scans or sensitive files are stored.
- Installable on phones and desktops as an app.
